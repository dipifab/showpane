# Showpane Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Showpane is a macOS app by Fabrizio Di Pietro. It shows a window of your choice inside its own
window, so you can share just that window in a video call.

## Data collected

None. Showpane does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no network access.

## Screen Recording permission

macOS requires the Screen Recording permission for Showpane to display another app's window.
Showpane uses it only to draw the window you choose, live, inside the Showpane window on your Mac.
Nothing is recorded: frames are never saved to disk or sent anywhere.

## Data stored on your Mac

Showpane remembers only the position and size of its own window, in its local preferences.
Deleting the app removes them.

## Third parties

Showpane shares no data with third parties. What you show in a meeting is handled by your video
call app, under that app's own privacy policy.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
